## Facebook

I built this Singapore Grand Prix dashboard with GPT-6.1-Sol, and I'm seriously impressed with what we made through iteration.

You can replay the completed 2026 race with recorded telemetry, explore a holographic Marina Bay, follow drivers in car cam, and inspect speed, gears, throttle and brake. The latest update adds detailed 3D F1 cars with textured tyres, suspension, a halo and proper wheel animation.

The small details took plenty of refinement: smooth camera flights between drivers, glowing telemetry charts, driver cards that follow off-screen cars, and track-shaped scan waves through the buildings. Sol-6.1 helped turn those requests into a working app, with tests and a live deployment.

This is why I'm excited about GPT-6.1-Sol as a coding model. I could keep refining the idea and see those changes become real.
