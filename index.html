    SG GRAND PRIX 2026 / DATA NOTES

    A circuit study after dark.

    The replay uses the 11 October 2026 Singapore Grand Prix, OpenF1 session 11388. All 22 drivers, lap and sector times, speed, gear, throttle, brake, DRS, standings, gaps, tyre stints and race-control messages come from that historical session. The archive retains approximately one telemetry sample per second. Missing samples display a dash. The 2026 source has no DRS records, so that status remains unavailable. Final standings include the official classification and penalties.

    Cars follow the recorded speed profile, integrated between samples and aligned to recorded lap crossings on the anti-clockwise circuit. A per-lap distance correction keeps the estimated position synchronized with timing. Missing speed intervals use lap timing. These positions are estimates, not recorded GPS trajectories. Speed charts show time through the selected lap. The weekend programme refers to the completed 2026 event. Post-race news links to official Formula 1 articles published after this race. The news is bundled at build time and makes no runtime API requests. Retired drivers stop at their last recorded moving telemetry sample and disappear from the circuit. An incomplete retirement lap uses integrated distance without scaling it to a full lap. The buildings and landmarks are procedural illustrations. Circuit coordinates are community mapping data. Surrounding road outlines follow OpenStreetMap road centerlines, with edge widths estimated from width, lane and road-class tags. Underground roads and footpaths are omitted. Track width, pit lane, barriers, grandstands, lights and sector divisions are schematic.
